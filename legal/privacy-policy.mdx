Cookies and similar technologies (including pixels, local storage, SDK identifiers, and tags) help us operate, secure, and improve Pocket.

### 9.1 What we use

- **Strictly necessary**: Required for core functionality such as session management, security, load balancing, and abuse prevention. Disabling these may break parts of the Services.
- **Performance**: Help us measure traffic, errors, and feature usage so we can improve stability and speed.
- **Functional**: Remember preferences such as language, display settings, or market favorites stored in combination with local storage.
- **Targeting / measurement**: Where enabled, help us measure campaigns and show relevant Pocket communications on our Platform or partner sites. Third-party ad or analytics partners may set their own cookies subject to their policies.

### 9.2 Duration

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain until they expire or you delete them.

### 9.3 Your choices

You may decline non-essential cookies through browser settings or in-product controls where offered. Blocking cookies may affect functionality.
