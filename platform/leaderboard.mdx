The Leaderboard ranks testnet traders by performance and activity. It is a **read-only showcase** in V2—no token prizes or referral incentives.

## Rankings

| Leaderboard | Metric |
|---|---|
| **PnL** | Realized + unrealized profit and loss over the selected window |
| **Volume** | Notional traded volume |

## Time filters

Typical windows (as shown in UI):

- 24H
- 7D
- 30D
- All time

## Search

Search by wallet address or display name (if shown) to jump to a specific rank.

## Data refresh

Leaderboard data refreshes at most **once per hour**. Rankings may lag live trading by up to ~60 minutes.

## Data source

Aggregations are computed server-side from onchain fills and position snapshots. Disputes should include wallet address and time range.

## Mainnet

Incentivized seasons and prizes are planned as separate growth modules—not part of testnet V2, and not tied to a platform token in this documentation.
