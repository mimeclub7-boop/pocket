The Leaderboard ranks testnet traders by performance and activity. Use it to compare yourself against other traders on Pocket Chain across rolling time windows.

<Note>
  Leaderboard is a **read-only showcase** in V2. There are no token prizes or referral incentives on testnet.
</Note>

## Rankings

| Leaderboard | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| **PnL** | Realized \+ unrealized profit and loss over the selected window |
| **Volume** | Notional traded volume |

## Time filters

Typical windows shown in the UI:

- 24H
- 7D
- 30D
- All time

## Search

Search by wallet address or display name (if shown) to jump directly to a specific rank.

<Info>
  Leaderboard data refreshes at most **once per hour**. Rankings may lag live trading by up to ~60 minutes.
</Info>

## Data source

Aggregations are computed server-side from onchain fills and position snapshots. Disputes should include wallet address and time range.

<Info>
  Incentivized seasons and prizes are planned as separate mainnet growth modules. They are not part of testnet V2 and are not tied to a platform token in this documentation.
</Info>
