The Announcements page publishes platform updates: maintenance windows, testnet resets, new markets, and incident postmortems.

## How to access

Top navigation → **Announcement**

## Page structure

| View | Content |
|---|---|
| List | Title, date, tag (e.g. Maintenance, Release, Incident) |
| Detail | Full markdown body, optional links |

## Tags

Common categories:

- **Release** — new features (V2 rollout, new order types)
- **Maintenance** — scheduled downtime
- **Incident** — unplanned outage summary and remediation
- **Testnet** — faucet resets, chain upgrades

## Notifications

V2 may surface an unread badge on the Announcement nav item when new posts ship. There is no email subscription in testnet V2.

## Operations

Content is managed internally (CMS or markdown in repo). For urgent issues, also check status via team channels.
