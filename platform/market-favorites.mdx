Pin frequently traded pairs to the **Favorites** tab in the market selector.

## Add a favorite

1. Open the market selector on the trade page
2. Find a pair in **All** or search
3. Click the star icon → pair moves to **Favorites**

## Remove a favorite

Open **Favorites** → click the filled star to unpin.

## Storage

Favorites are stored **locally in your browser** (per device). Clearing site data removes your list.

## Sync across devices

Favorites do not sync across browsers or between wallet and email login accounts in V2. Server-side watchlists may come in a later release.

## Mobile

Favorites work on mobile web inside the same market selector drawer.
