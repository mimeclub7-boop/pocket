---

Pin frequently traded pairs to the **Favorites** tab in the market selector. Favorites give you a personal shortcut list on the trade page so you can jump between the pairs you follow most.

## Add a favorite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the market selector">
    Click the market name on the trade page to open the selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a pair">
    Browse **All** or use search to locate the pair.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin it">
    Click the star icon next to the pair. It moves to **Favorites**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a favorite

Open the **Favorites** tab and click the filled star to unpin.

<Warning>
  Favorites are stored **locally in your browser** on the current device. Clearing site data removes your list.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Favorites do not sync across browsers or between wallet and email login accounts in V2. Server-side watchlists may come in a later release.
</Info>

## Mobile

Favorites work on mobile web inside the same market selector drawer.
