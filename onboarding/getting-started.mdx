This guide covers the **Testnet V1 + V2** experience on Pocket. Testnet uses simulated funds only—there is no real deposit or withdrawal.

## Who this is for

Testnet is currently an **internal validation environment** (team + market makers). Access may be invite-only depending on deployment settings.

## End-to-end flow

```
Connect wallet (or email login)
  → Accept Terms of Use (V2 onboarding)
  → Enable Trading
  → Claim testnet tokens (Faucet)
  → Trade spot or perps
  → Review portfolio & onchain records
```

## Step-by-step

### 1. Open the app

Go to [pocket.fi/trade](https://pocket.fi/trade). You can browse markets before connecting a wallet.

### 2. Connect or sign in

- **Wallet**: MetaMask, WalletConnect, OKX Wallet, or Coinbase Wallet
- **Email (V2)**: Email + OTP via Privy; an embedded wallet is created automatically

See [Connect your wallet](/onboarding/connect-wallet) and [Email login](/onboarding/email-login).

### 3. Complete onboarding (V2)

New users see a combined **Welcome + Terms of Use** modal. You must accept the [Terms of Use](/legal/terms-of-use) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) before trading.

After acceptance, follow **Enable Trading**:

1. **Claim testnet tokens** — default allocation is test USDC
2. **Establish connection** — sign once to create a local trading key (gas-free)

### 4. Claim tokens

If you skipped the onboarding faucet step, use the standalone [Testnet faucet](/onboarding/testnet-faucet) page or the in-trade faucet entry.

### 5. Place your first trade

- Pick a market from the selector (use [Favorites](/platform/market-favorites) to pin pairs)
- Choose **Spot** or **Perps**
- Submit a **Market** or **Limit** order
- Confirm fills in the bottom panel tabs

### 6. Verify onchain

Search your transaction hash in [Clob Scan](/explorers/clob-scan) or open [Pocket Scan](/explorers/pocket-scan) for full chain data.

## What testnet does not include

| Feature | Status on testnet |
|---|---|
| Real deposit / withdraw | Not available — use Faucet |
| Vaults | Mainnet only |
| Referrals / points | Mainnet only |
| Platform token / staking | Not launched — omitted from these docs |
