Testnet V2 adds **email login** so users can trade without installing a browser wallet extension.

Pocket integrates **Privy** for email authentication. After verifying your email, Privy creates an embedded wallet tied to your address so you can trade immediately.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Email">
    Click **Connect Wallet**, then choose **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type the email address you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the OTP">
    Type the one-time passcode sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Embedded wallet created">
    Privy creates an embedded wallet tied to your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Trading">
    Continue with **Enable Trading** as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Embedded wallet behavior

| Topic | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Custody | Embedded wallet keys are managed by Privy; export options follow Privy policy |
| Same features | Spot, perps, portfolio, leaderboard—same as extension wallets |
| Onchain address | You receive a normal Pocket Chain address visible in Portfolio |

## When to use email vs extension wallet

| Use email if… | Use extension wallet if… |
| --- | --- |
| You want the fastest first-time setup | You already use MetaMask / OKX daily |
| You're on mobile without a wallet app | You need hardware wallet support |
| You're doing a quick testnet demo | You prefer full self-custody from day one |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="OTP not received">
    Check your spam folder, wait 60 seconds, then request a new code.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Session expired">
    Log in again and re-run Enable Trading if prompted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Want to switch to MetaMask later">
    Connect MetaMask as a new address. Accounts are separate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
