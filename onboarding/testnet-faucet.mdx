The faucet distributes **test tokens only**. They have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn to other networks.

## Default allocation

When you claim through **Enable Trading**, you typically receive:

| Asset | Amount (testnet default) |
|---|---|
| USDC | 1,000 on first claim; 100 every 24h thereafter |

Exact amounts may change per deployment. The UI always shows the current quota.

## How to claim

### During onboarding

1. Connect wallet → **Enable Trading**
2. Step 1/2: click **Claim** on the faucet step
3. Wait for confirmation → proceed to Establish Connection

### Standalone faucet page

1. Navigate to **Faucet** from the top navigation
2. Connect wallet if needed
3. Click **Claim**
4. Balances update in Portfolio and the trade panel

## Claim limits

- First claim and daily top-up follow the amounts shown in the UI
- If you already claimed today, the UI shows a completed or countdown state instead of the claim button

## Send tokens (testnet)

After claiming, you can transfer test assets to another Pocket address via **Send Tokens** (Portfolio or trade UI). This helps multi-account testing.

## Not a deposit

The faucet replaces deposit/withdraw on testnet. Real USDC bridging will be documented separately at mainnet launch.
