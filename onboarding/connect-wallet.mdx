Pocket is non-custodial. You connect a wallet you control, and Pocket never holds your private keys.

<Note>
  Pocket does not store or manage your seed phrase. Your wallet provider remains the sole custodian of your keys.
</Note>

## Supported wallets

| Wallet | Connection method |
| --- | --- |
| MetaMask | Browser extension / in-app browser |
| WalletConnect | QR code or deep link |
| OKX Wallet | Extension or mobile |
| Coinbase Wallet | Extension or mobile |

## Connect flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect Wallet">
    Click **Connect Wallet** in the top bar or on the trade panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your wallet">
    Choose your wallet provider from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection">
    Approve the connection request in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm connection">
    Your address appears in the top bar when connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enable Trading

Connecting a wallet alone does not authorize trading. Click **Enable Trading** to complete setup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Claim testnet tokens">
    Claim your testnet tokens if you have not already done so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign to connect">
    Sign a gas-free message to establish a local trading session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The session can stay connected across visits if you opt in to **Stay Connected**.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Wallet not detected">
    Install the extension or open the site inside the wallet's in-app browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Connection loop">
    Disconnect on pocket.fi, reset the wallet connection, then reconnect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong network">
    Switch to the Pocket Chain testnet network shown in the wallet prompt.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Enable Trading stuck">
    Refresh the page, ensure pop-up blockers are off, and retry the signature.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security tips

<Warning>
  Never share your seed phrase or private key.
</Warning>

- Verify the URL is `pocket.fi` before signing
- Disconnect when using a shared device

For passwordless access, see [Email login](/onboarding/email-login).
