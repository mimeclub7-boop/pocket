**Pocket Scan** is the public block explorer for Pocket Chain. It gives you a complete view of network activity outside the trading interface so you can verify blocks, transactions, addresses, and contract interactions independently.

## What you can look up

- Blocks and block producers
- Transactions and internal transfers
- Wallet addresses and token balances
- Contract interactions (as indexed)

## How to open from Pocket

Testnet V2 adds Pocket Scan to the top navigation:

**Explorer** dropdown → **Pocket Scan** (opens the explorer in a new tab)

## Clob Scan vs Pocket Scan

|  | Clob Scan | Pocket Scan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Location | Inside pocket.fi | External explorer |
| Best for | Order book trades & clearing | General L1 exploration |
| Audience | Traders verifying fills | Developers, auditors, power users |

<Warning>
  Confirm you are viewing the correct network in Pocket Scan (testnet vs mainnet). Network labels are shown in the explorer header.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Pocket's positioning is **verifiable fairness**: any fill you see in the app should be traceable to an onchain record in Clob Scan or Pocket Scan.
</Tip>
