Testnet V2 lets you generate a **shareable PnL poster** from an open or recently closed position.

## How to share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Positions or Trade History">
    Navigate to the **Positions** or **Trade History** tab in the bottom panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Share icon">
    Click the **Share** icon on the position row you want to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the poster">
    Review the generated poster, which shows the pair, side, PnL percentage, and entry versus mark price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Pick one of the following options:

    - **Copy image** to clipboard
    - **Download** PNG
    - **Share link**, which opens the native share sheet on supported mobile browsers
  </Step>

## Poster content

Typical fields on the poster:

- Trading pair and direction (Long / Short)
- Return percentage and optional absolute PnL
- Entry vs mark/exit prices
- Pocket branding watermark

Sticker variants may be available in the picker UI.

## Privacy

<Note>
  Posters show **only what you choose to share**. They do not expose your wallet address unless the template includes it. Check the preview before posting.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Copy fails">
    Use Download instead; some browsers block clipboard image writes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Blank poster">
    Refresh position data and ensure the mark price is loaded before generating the poster.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong PnL">
    Confirm you selected the correct closed fill versus open position.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
