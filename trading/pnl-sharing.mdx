Testnet V2 lets you generate a **shareable PnL poster** from an open or recently closed position.

## How to share

1. Open **Positions** or **Trade History**
2. Click the **Share** icon on a position row
3. Preview the generated poster (pair, side, PnL %, entry/mark)
4. Choose an action:
   - **Copy image** to clipboard
   - **Download** PNG
   - **Share link** (opens native share sheet on supported mobile browsers)

## Poster content

Typical fields on the poster:

- Trading pair and direction (Long / Short)
- Return percentage and optional absolute PnL
- Entry vs mark/exit prices
- Pocket branding watermark

Sticker variants may be available in the picker UI.

## Privacy

Posters show **only what you choose to share**. They do not expose your wallet address unless the template includes it—check the preview before posting.

## Referral links (mainnet)

On mainnet, share URLs may append a `join/{CODE}` referral parameter for users who have opened a referral code. Referral mechanics are documented separately when launched.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Copy fails | Use Download instead; some browsers block clipboard image writes |
| Blank poster | Refresh position data; ensure mark price is loaded |
| Wrong PnL | Confirm you selected the correct closed fill vs open position |
